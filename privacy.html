Credentials Manager - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Credentials Manager is a macOS app that keeps tokens, API keys, and passwords in an encrypted vault on your Mac. It has no server and no account system of its own. This page explains what the app does with your Google data when you turn on Google Drive sync.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (scope drive.file, the only scope the app requests). The app creates one file, vault.cmv, in a "Credentials Manager" folder in your Drive, and reads and updates only that file. It cannot see any other file in your Drive, your email address, or your profile.

How your data is protected

  • The vault is encrypted on your Mac (AES-256-GCM) before it is written to disk or uploaded. The file in your Drive contains only ciphertext; neither Google nor the developer can read your credentials.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac only. It is never uploaded, never stored in the vault, and never sent anywhere except to Google to authorize Drive requests.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app talks only to Google's sign-in and Drive services.
  • Your data is never sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than syncing your own vault between your own Macs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Credentials Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Sign out in the app's settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "Credentials Manager" folder from your Google Drive to remove the encrypted copy. The vault on your Mac is unaffected.
